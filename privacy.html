Mental Math · Privacy Policy

Mental Math · Support

Last updated: 16 November 2026. This policy covers both apps.

Mental Math collects no data

Mental Math for Learners and Mental Math for Teachers do not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the apps contain no third-party code that collects data. They do not use the internet at all.

What stays on your device

Your practice statistics, contest scores and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Reset progress on the Progress page clears them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Printed tests (Mental Math for Teachers)

Test papers and answer keys are made as PDF files on your device. They go only where you send them from the share sheet (a printer, Files, AirDrop, Mail and so on); we never see them.

Purchases

Mental Math for Learners is free. Mental Math for Teachers is bought once from Apple's App Store; Apple handles the payment and we never see your payment details or your Apple Account. Neither app has in-app purchases.

Apple's own privacy policy applies to the App Store: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Links

A note at the bottom of some pages opens the App Store page of our other app, Hexagon Logic Puzzles. It is the only link out of the apps.

Children

Both apps are suitable for all ages. Because they collect no data at all, they collect none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
